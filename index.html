Sudoku Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Daily handles information in the current version of the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Sudoku Daily uses local storage to support app features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include game progress, saved puzzles, game history, statistics, custom puzzles, and app preferences such as theme and notification settings. This information is stored on your device and is not automatically sent to the developer.

2. Accounts

The current version does not require an account or sign-in to use its core features.

3. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Sudoku Daily may schedule local reminders or daily challenge notifications on your device. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

4. Advertising and Advertising Identifiers

The version of Sudoku Daily covered by this policy does not include Google AdMob or the Google Mobile Ads SDK and does not display in-app advertisements. It does not intentionally request or use the Android advertising ID for ad delivery in this version.

If a future version adds advertising or another service that changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices before that version is released.

5. How Information Is Used

Information stored by Sudoku Daily is used to provide app features such as saving and restoring game progress, maintaining history and statistics, remembering preferences, supporting daily challenges, and scheduling notifications you enable. The current version does not use in-app advertising, ad personalization, or ad measurement.

6. Sharing of Information

Sudoku Daily does not operate an account service that automatically sends your locally stored game progress, history, statistics, or preferences to the developer. We do not sell your personal information. If you choose to share content or open an external link using a feature of your device, the app or website you select may process information you provide; its own privacy policy applies.

7. Security and Retention

Locally stored app information may remain on your device until you remove it using available app features, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. The developer does not operate an account database for the local game information described in this policy. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Daily is a puzzle app and does not require an account to use its core features. The app's intended audience must be reflected accurately in its store listing and release configuration. If the app is offered to children or is otherwise subject to child-directed privacy requirements, its practices must comply with applicable law and platform policies.

9. Your Choices

You can manage notification permissions in your device settings and remove locally stored app data through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app. You can choose whether to use device sharing features or open external links.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, third-party services, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are made. If advertising or another service is added in a future release, this policy will be updated to reflect the applicable data practices.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at sudokudaily.support@gmail.com.
