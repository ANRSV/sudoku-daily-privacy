Sudoku Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Sudoku Daily stores on your device and what information may be processed by Google Mobile Ads, Google Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics when the relevant features are enabled.

1. Information stored on your device

Sudoku Daily uses local on-device storage for core app features. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • Active puzzle state, entered values, notes, and move history.
  • Completed game records, times, hints, mistakes, streaks, and aggregate statistics.
  • Custom puzzles created in the app.
  • Your locally saved profile display name.
  • App preferences such as theme, appearance, sound, vibration, and notification schedule.

The inspected app build uses local database and device-preference storage. It does not provide a Sudoku Daily account system or first-party cloud account sync. Local storage should not be understood as a guarantee of encryption or secure-vault protection.

2. Accounts and authentication

You can use the app without creating an account, registering, signing in, or providing an email address. The profile display name, if entered, is stored locally on your device.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules notifications locally through the device notification system. Notification permission can be changed in your device settings. The inspected project does not implement a first-party push-notification server.

4. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The inspected project contains banner, interstitial, and rewarded-ad implementations. Ads are requested only when the app’s consent service indicates that ad requests are permitted.

When an ad is requested, Google and participating advertising technology providers may process information such as IP address, advertising or app/device identifiers where available, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. The precise data processed can depend on device settings, consent choices, ad format, region, and Google’s current services.

Advertising partners process information under their own privacy terms. Review Google’s policy and AdMob disclosures using the links below.

5. Consent and privacy choices (Google UMP)

The project uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) through the Google Mobile Ads integration. At app startup, the app refreshes consent information and presents a consent form when Google determines one is required. Ad requests remain disabled unless the consent service reports that they are permitted.

Where Google UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, the app displays “Privacy Choices” in Settings. Use that entry to review or change the advertising privacy choices presented by Google. The availability and content of consent controls can vary by region and applicable law.

6. Firebase Analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are enabled by default, subject to applicable regional requirements to help us understand app usage and diagnose technical problems. You can independently turn either service off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data → Data & Diagnostics. If you turn a service off, the app asks that SDK to stop future collection; information already transmitted to Google cannot be recalled from Google's systems.

We use Analytics to measure aggregate feature engagement, understand which game modes and difficulty levels are used, and improve app usability. We use Crashlytics to identify crash causes, affected app/device versions, and stability trends. We do not use these services to create a Sudoku Daily sign-in account; the app does not offer first-party registration or sign-in.

While enabled, Google Analytics may process app usage events, app/device information, and identifiers used by the service. Firebase Crashlytics may process crash traces, app state, and device/app diagnostics needed to diagnose failures. These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google’s privacy terms.

Sudoku Daily does not have a sign-in account or first-party user profile database, and we do not intentionally send your locally saved profile name or an account ID to Firebase. The Firebase SDKs may use app-instance or device/app identifiers to associate diagnostics or usage events from the same app installation. This is not the same as linking those events to a Sudoku Daily registration account.

The app does not intentionally attach your profile display name, email address, entered puzzle digits, notes, or full puzzle state to custom Analytics events or Crashlytics keys. Diagnostics may still include technical details generated by the SDK and operating system.

Turning a setting off stops future collection after the SDK applies the change. Crash reports already stored locally by the Crashlytics SDK may be retained on the device and could be uploaded if collection is enabled again later. The app cannot directly delete data already received by Google.

7. How information is used

  • To run Sudoku gameplay, save progress, and provide game history and statistics.
  • To remember your preferences and local notification schedule.
  • To request and display ads when permitted by consent and applicable settings.
  • To help advertising providers measure ad delivery, prevent invalid activity, and maintain service security.
  • To understand app feature engagement and diagnose crashes and stability issues through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

The developer does not operate a first-party account database for the app in the inspected project. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Retention and deletion

Game progress, profile name, preferences, and statistics remain in local app storage until you use the app’s available reset controls, clear the app’s storage through your device settings, or uninstall the app. Device-level backups may be governed by your operating-system and backup settings.

Information processed by Google or other ad technology providers is retained and deleted according to those providers’ policies and applicable legal requirements. The developer cannot directly delete data held independently by those providers; use the provider privacy controls or contact the provider.

9. Children’s privacy

Sudoku Daily is declared for an 18-and-over target audience in Google Play Console and is not designed or directed specifically at children. The store content rating (currently 3+) describes content suitability and is separate from the target-audience declaration. The app does not use an age-gate. The publisher must keep its target-audience declaration accurate if the app’s design or marketing changes.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact the support address below.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Use Settings → Privacy & Data → Data & Diagnostics to independently manage Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics opt-in.
  • Use Settings → Privacy Choices when that option is shown to manage Google advertising consent.
  • Change or reset your device advertising ID using your device’s operating-system settings.
  • Change notification permissions in app or device settings.
  • Use the app’s reset controls, clear app storage, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.

Depending on your location, privacy laws may provide rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. Requests about information processed by advertising providers may need to be sent to those providers.

Regional consent and diagnostics controls

Where Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents a regional consent message, Google Analytics consent settings are applied through Google Consent Mode when that feature is enabled for this app in AdMob. If the required consent refresh cannot be completed, Sudoku Daily keeps Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection disabled for that app launch.

Crash reports are a separate diagnostics purpose and are not treated as covered by advertising consent. Where UMP indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, Crashlytics remains disabled until you explicitly enable it in Usage & Diagnostics, unless you have already made that explicit choice. You can turn Analytics and Crashlytics off independently in the app settings.

Regional requirements differ. This app's controls do not replace the privacy rights and notices required by the laws that apply where you live.
