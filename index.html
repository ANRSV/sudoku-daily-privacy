Sudoku Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Daily handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Sudoku Daily uses local storage to support app features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include game progress, saved puzzles, game history, statistics, custom puzzles, and app preferences such as theme and notification settings. This locally stored app information is kept on your device and is not automatically sent to us.

2. Accounts

The current version does not require an account or sign-in to use its core features.

3. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Sudoku Daily may schedule local reminders or daily challenge notifications. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Sudoku Daily uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. When ads are requested or displayed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share certain information with Google, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and the features used. This may include your IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), advertising or other device identifiers, information about interactions with the app or ads, and diagnostic information about app or SDK performance. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Google handles information collected through its advertising services under its own policies. For more information, read the Google Privacy Policy and Google's information about advertising. You can also review advertising and privacy controls available on your Android device and in Google settings.

5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Sudoku Daily may be used to save and restore game progress, maintain history and statistics, remember preferences, provide daily challenges, and support notifications you enable. Information handled by Google AdMob may also be used for ad delivery, advertising personalization where permitted, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, according to Google's policies and applicable settings and consent choices.

6. Sharing of Information

Sudoku Daily does not operate an account service that sends your locally stored game progress, history, or preferences to us. However, information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be transmitted to and processed by Google and its service providers for advertising-related purposes described above. The data handled can depend on the SDK version, device settings, consent choices, and ad features used. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Security and Retention

Locally stored information may remain on your device until you remove it using available app features, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Information processed by Google through its advertising services is subject to Google's retention and security practices. Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Daily is a puzzle app. The app's intended audience and applicable child-directed privacy and advertising requirements must be reflected accurately in its store listing and release configuration. Advertising and data practices may be limited or configured differently where required by applicable law or platform policy.

9. Your Choices

You can manage notification permissions in your device settings and remove locally stored app data through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app. You can also use available Android and Google settings to manage advertising privacy and personalization choices. Where consent is required for advertising, the app's consent flow and ad configuration should be used to record and apply those choices.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, third-party services, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at sudokudaily.support@gmail.com.
