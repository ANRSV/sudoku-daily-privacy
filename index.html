Sudoku Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Daily handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Sudoku Daily uses local storage to support app features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include game progress, saved puzzles, game history, statistics, custom puzzles, and app preferences such as theme and notification settings. This locally stored app information is kept on your device and is not automatically sent to us.

2. Accounts

The current version does not require an account or sign-in to use its core features.

3. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Sudoku Daily may schedule local reminders or daily challenge notifications. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Sudoku Daily is a free Sudoku puzzle game supported by advertisements provided through Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The app does not sell coins, offer gambling, or process payments for in-game currency.

When the advertising SDK makes ad requests or displays ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and information needed to deliver, measure, and protect advertising against fraud or abuse. The exact information processed can depend on the SDK version, device and Android settings, the ad format, and the consent and ad-request configuration in effect.

Ads and the data used for advertising may be limited or handled differently for children, mixed audiences, or users in regions where specific protections or consent are required. The app must apply the applicable Google Play Families and Google Mobile Ads requirements in its actual implementation; this policy does not itself configure ad requests or establish that those technical safeguards are enabled.

Google's handling of information is also governed by Google's policies. Read the Google Privacy Policy and Google's information about advertising. You can review advertising and privacy controls available on your Android device and in Google settings.

5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Sudoku Daily may be used to save and restore game progress, maintain history and statistics, remember preferences, provide daily challenges, and support notifications you enable. Information handled by Google AdMob may also be used for ad delivery, advertising personalization where permitted, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, according to Google's policies and applicable settings and consent choices.

6. Sharing of Information

Sudoku Daily does not operate an account service and does not send your locally stored game progress, history, or preferences to us as part of normal gameplay. Information handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be transmitted to Google and its service providers for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes, as described above. The categories and processing can vary with the SDK version, device settings, ad formats, consent choices, and the app's ad-request configuration. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Security and Retention

Locally stored information may remain on your device until you remove it using available app features, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Information processed by Google through its advertising services is subject to Google's retention and security practices. Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Daily is a general-purpose Sudoku puzzle game intended to be suitable for people of all ages, including children. The game does not contain adult content, gambling, or real-money gameplay features. The app does not require an account or ask users to provide their name, email address, or other personal details to play.

The app uses third-party advertising services. Those services may process device and advertising information as described in the Advertising and Third-Party Services section. We do not knowingly use Sudoku Daily's own gameplay storage to collect personal information directly from children. This statement does not mean that third-party advertising SDKs collect no information.

Because children may use the app, the app's Google Play target-audience settings, advertising SDK configuration, ad content rating, consent handling, and any required age-screening or Families safeguards must be configured to meet applicable Google Play and legal requirements. These technical settings are managed in the app and Play Console and are not established by this policy text alone. If you believe information has been handled inappropriately, contact us using the details below.

9. Your Choices

You can manage notification permissions in your device settings and remove locally stored app data through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app. You can use available Android and Google settings to manage advertising privacy and personalization choices. Where consent is required, the app's consent flow and advertising configuration must apply the relevant choices before making ad requests. The availability of particular controls can depend on your location, device, and Google settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, third-party services, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at sudokudaily.support@gmail.com.
