Sudoku Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Daily handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Sudoku Daily uses local storage to support app features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include game progress, saved puzzles, game history, statistics, custom puzzles, and app preferences such as theme and notification settings.

2. Accounts

The current version does not require an account or sign-in to use its core features.

3. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Sudoku Daily may schedule local reminders or daily challenge notifications. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Google AdMob is not integrated in the current app version. If advertising or other third-party services are added, this policy will be updated to describe the services used and the information they may collect, use, or share. Third-party services may handle information under their own privacy policies.

5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the app may be used to save and restore game progress, maintain history and statistics, remember preferences, provide daily challenges, and support notifications you enable.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information stored locally is not automatically sent to us simply because it is stored on your device. The app's final third-party services and data practices must be reviewed before release.

7. Security and Retention

Locally stored information may remain on your device until you remove it using available app features, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. We encourage you to use your device's security features. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Daily is a puzzle app. Its intended audience and any applicable child-directed privacy and advertising requirements will be confirmed for the release version.

9. Your Choices

You can manage notification permissions in your device settings and remove locally stored app data through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, third-party services, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at sudokudaily.support@gmail.com.
